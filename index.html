About me

I'm studying for a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at St. Dominic College of Asia and working toward a career in full-stack development. I design, build and animate websites, and I've learned most of what I know by building things and pushing myself to the limit.

Alongside HTML, CSS and JavaScript, I also work with Java, C and Dart.

Behind the camera, I have three years of photography experience and two years of videography. I shoot landscapes, people and events, but aviation is the subject I keep coming back to.

When I'm not spotting planes from the ground, I'm flying them in a simulator.
